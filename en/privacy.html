Privacy Policy

for the Weckbuddy app · Last updated: October 7, 2026

Summary

Weckbuddy does not collect, store or transmit any personal data to us or to third parties. The app has no user account, no ads, no tracking and no analytics tools. Everything you enter in the app stays on your device.

2. What data does the app process?

The app stores the following exclusively on your device:

  • your alarms (time, label, days of the week, snooze setting),
  • your buddies (name, purpose, chosen character, awake or asleep),
  • a technical setting indicating whether the starter buddies have already been created.

This data never leaves your device and cannot be seen by us. If you delete the app, this data is deleted too. It may be part of your personal iCloud or device backup, which Apple manages according to your own settings.

3. “Alarms” permission

So that your alarms ring even in silent mode, the app asks once for permission to set alarms (Apple AlarmKit). The alarms are managed by your device’s operating system. No data is transmitted to us. You can revoke this permission at any time in your device’s settings.

4. Children and teenagers

Weckbuddy is designed for children and teenagers. That is exactly why we completely refrain from collecting personal data and from ads, in-app purchases and connections to social networks.

5. App Store

The app is distributed through Apple’s App Store. When you download it, Apple processes data under its own responsibility, such as your Apple Account. We have no influence on this. You can find more information in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

6. Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Since we do not store any data about you, we usually cannot provide information about stored data – but feel free to contact us with any questions.

7. Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects how data is processed, we will update this privacy policy.

The German version of this privacy policy is authoritative.
