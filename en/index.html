Weckbuddy

A simple alarm clock with buddies – for kids and teens.

Frequently asked questions

Does the alarm ring in silent mode?

Yes. When you set your first alarm, your iPhone asks whether Weckbuddy may set alarms. If you allow it, alarms ring even in silent mode and with Focus turned on – just like the built-in Clock app.

I declined the permission. What now?

Open Settings → Apps → Weckbuddy and allow “Alarms” there. Then turn the alarm back on in the app.

What are buddies?

A buddy stands for a situation, such as school, weekend, holidays or sick days. Each buddy can have several alarms. Tap the paw at the top of the app to rename buddies, change their look or create new ones.

Why is a buddy asleep?

Tap a buddy at the top of your alarm list to put it to sleep. Sleeping buddies won’t wake you, but their alarms are kept. Tap the buddy again to wake it up – for example, put Pip (school) to sleep and wake Bruno (sick) when you stay home sick.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. All alarms and buddies stay on your device. Learn more in the privacy policy.
